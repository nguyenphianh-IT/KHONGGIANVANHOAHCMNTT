Evidence

PHASE_0B_2_3_ROUND3_8_HANDOVER.md, Section 9 reports: E20F2E63C7B94F2911F3B03A5EBE7AF68F6E7BBA59383D1B948653639EB4E48A.

PHASE_0B_2_3_ROUND3_8_REMEDIATION_REPORT.md, Section 7 reports the same E20F...E48A value.

The separate Project Team result pasted outside the ZIP reported 8608A907...982A8D1, matching the archive, but that does not correct the contradictory values embedded in the delivered package.

Impact: The handover and remediation report do not seal the actual archive. The critical Round 4.7 checksum reconciliation objective is therefore not fully satisfied for Round 3.8.

Status: FAIL – CRITICAL.

R4.8-02 – HIGH: Manifest Summary and Inventory Metadata Are Internally Inconsistent

Evidence

The manifest table contains 42 filename rows, and all 42 names match ZIP entries.

The manifest summary labels the Architecture &amp; Design group as 5 files but lists 8 filenames.

The summary claims 30 historical dossier files, which does not reconcile with the listed 42-entry inventory and the other stated group counts.

The four core specifications are listed as a four-file group in the summary, but the inventory's origin/category labels do not consistently classify all four as that group.

The manifest row for PHASE_0B_2_3_ROUND3_8_MANIFEST.md states 2 lines; the actual file contains 92 text lines (93 if counting a terminal empty line).

Other listed line counts appear consistently one higher than splitlines() counts, which may reflect a different counting convention; the manifest does not define that convention. This is not independently treated as a separate defect for every row, but the convention should be documented.

Impact: Filename-level reconciliation passes, but category totals and manifest metadata are not reliable as currently presented.

Status: PARTIAL / REWORK REQUIRED.

R4.8-03 – HIGH: Evidence Provenance and Remediation Claims Are Not Fully Reconciled

Evidence

The Round 3.8 report and handover still contain phrases such as “loại bỏ triệt để”, “khắc phục toàn diện”, “giải quyết dứt điểm”, “nhất quán tuyệt đối” when describing the remediation process. In several places these are quoted as historical wording being removed, so their presence alone is not proof of a current completion claim. However, the report should clearly label these as quoted legacy phrases, not use them as affirmative descriptions of the Round 3.8 work.

Historical Round 3.1 and 3.2 delta reports retain assertions including ALL 6 AUDIT FINDINGS ... FULLY RESOLVED and ALL 6 FINDINGS FULLY RESOLVED / ZERO REGRESSION. These may be historical project-team statements, but the current package does not consistently qualify them as historical self-assessments rather than current independent findings.

The Round 3.8 report says the full dossier was reviewed and the overstatement language removed, but the retained historical records are not consistently annotated with provenance and temporal scope.

Impact: Evidence provenance is improved, but the claim that the entire dossier has been fully normalized is not sufficiently demonstrated.

Status: PARTIAL.

R4.8-04 – MEDIUM: Quality Gate Independent-Verification Labels Need Evidence-Bounded Scope

Evidence

The 21/21 Document-Level PASS is explicitly labelled Project Team Self-Assessment. This distinction is accepted.

The independent-verification column labels QG-01 through QG-04 as independently verified in earlier archive-integrity rounds. Archive integrity checks do not, by themselves, establish semantic correctness of the four core specifications or prove harmonization of all 36 Acceptance Criteria.

The cited historical audit rounds may contain additional evidence, but those audit reports are not included in this 42-file package. The claims cannot be re-established from the Round 3.8 archive alone.

QG-05 is attributed to Round 4.1 historical audit. This is a historical carry-forward claim, not a fresh Round 4.8 verification.

Impact: The table should distinguish “historically reported as verified in a prior audit” from “reverified in Round 4.8”. Do not imply archive integrity independently proves semantic or requirement-level conformity.

Status: PARTIAL.

R4.8-05 – LOW: Proposed Workflow Is Clearly Non-Operational, With a Small Residual Wording Risk

Evidence

The Media Library Specification explicitly identifies the pipeline as proposed, not implemented, and not verified.

Step 6 is marked as blocked due to the unverified media_assets schema dependency.

The specification states that retries, rollback, compensation, locks, and cleanup are design proposals, not operational controls.

The mandatory media and idempotency blocker states remain present.

Impact: The principal non-operational boundary is adequately documented. Some workflow steps describe proposed actions in imperative technical language; retaining the nearby “proposed design only” banners is essential.
